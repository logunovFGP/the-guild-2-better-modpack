MegaModPack Reforged - Lite 0.1

General

- Script cleanup: Corrected tabs and spaces in scripts. Added sections and comments. Optimized structure and functions.
- Script cleanup: Removed redundand impacts and other unused DB entries.
- Seperated unused scripts inside the new 'Removed' folder (To make use of that, you must delete your original Scripts folder before applying this patch).
- Removed unnecessary scripts seperation for cutscenes in multiple cases.
- Script cleanup: Changed SetRepeatTimer to SetMeasureRepeat during multiple measures.
- Newly hired workers will no longer get cured automaticly.
- Added the original ships of Pirates of the Eastern Seas (and thus ships show their dynasty color again).
- Removed the button 'Remove from party' inside the family tree because that button removed a character permanently. Use the measure 'Measure group' instead for adding and removing as you like.
- The option for frequency of office sessions during game setup has been removed from multiplayer because it lead to an OOS in the second turn. Default setting is 2 now.
- The idle and freetime behavior of many NPCs has been altered either to improve performance or immersion.
- You can no longer marry workers or guards.

Bugfixes

- Multiple artefact-scripts didn't use the correct item name from the DB.
- Exploit: After starting certain artefact measures, you could remove the item from the inventory before it was deleted and still get the full effect.
- The poisoned cake didn't lower the fighting skill.
- The measure 'Assign for treatment' could get stuck. Also the script has been optimized.
- The measure 'Use thesis paper' could not start properly due to a critical bug.
- The measure 'Ask around' could lead to a freeze. Also you did not get evidence properly.
- The measure 'Make a present' had no cooldown.
- The measure 'Ask someone to dance' could have the wrong cooldown. It's now linked to the DB correctly.
- The measure 'Imperial politics' had no cooldown.
- Due to a copy and paste error the measure 'Use bone bangle' could have the wrong cooldown.
- Shadow AI could not start the game as an alchemist.
- Fixed multiple DB entries regarding background music in buildings.
- AI could get stuck in taverns during the 'sweet talking' measure. Fixed multiple additional issues.
- Removed the cooldown of the "train" measure. Fixed the pathfinding during that measure to prevent characters moving through the chimney.
- There was a small bug inside the AI if they could control the buisness inspectors.
- The sorcerer's robe had no state icon.
- Improved the workaround against spinning carts (stucking forever) with a more effective solution which is also better for the overall game performance.
- You can no longer hire workers who are currently courted (prevents bugs).
- The automatic search for high level workers did not work.
- High level workers no longer lose their XP if they get hired.
- Neutral traders and ships would do nothing after a few rounds. Also fixed some script errors.
- War happened only a few times in the beginning of the game. 
- During war, your actions would have no effect on victory or defeat no matter how many troops you would get. 
- The fix against the exploit of fire and courting employees has been improved.
- AI Pirate ships no longer attack fishing boats
- If you tear down or sell your bank you get back your credit account money.
- Exploit: If you tear down your building you now only get back half the value.

Features

- Your medical treatments slightly boost the opinion of the patients to the owner of that hospital
- Improved hospital AI: No more than 2 employees will be assigned to treat patients.
- Added a bigger world map.
- Added a bigger building upgrade sheet.
- Improved some female character textures.
- The variance of music pieces has been boosted massivly. Also about 20 songs from the Original Europa 1400: The Guild have been added to the game!
- The famous trading routes of ThreeOfMe have been added.

Balancing

- Sweet talking needs 300 instead of 200 Gold. Tavern keepers will get that money.
- Church owners will get the money from changing faith.
- Favor gain of sweet talking has been altered.
- You can now use ALL of your buisness inspectors to inspect buisnesses instead of just one at a time. However you can no longer inspect buisnesses more than once every 48 hours.
  Using that power will make you lose a lot of favor with the owner of that buisness and with their workers.
- Sorcerer's robe no longer will give you a malus.
- Declining a bribe will now lose you only 5 favor.
