--Project Description: --

Fajeth's MegaModPack Reforged is an extensive non-commercial Fan-Addon for The Guild 2 - Renaissance. It fixes hundreds of smaller and larger errors of the original game and 
improves the logic and performance of the code underneath it. Furthermore, the Addon extends the game in both depth and width. New features, for example new items
or interactions, are seamlessly integrated with the original, thus putting a new spin on a beloved classic. 

The addon is a 'Total-Conversion'-Mod, meaning a general overhaul of almost all parts of the game. Besides the sometimes massive changes, the charm, style and feel of the
original The Guild 2 has been kept and strengthened. Where features of the original were rather flat or shallow due to time constraints, the mod offers additional
depth of gameplay and immersive additions. None of the changes will break that original feel of the vanilla game.

Besides all of this, two major philosophies guide our approach: firstly, the Modpack tries to lower the barrier of entry new players are confronted with. More intuitive HUD elements and visualisations (as far as possible),
improved tooltips, new tips and tricks, and additional feedback messages during actions serve to convey the functionality of many game elements (both old or new) in a much clearer way with a more
reasonable learning curve.
Secondly, a big focus has been the development of the AI, and even better: interactions with the AI. A believable and enjoyable AI opponent is as important as a reliable and controllable system for
managing your workshop administrations. These elements are the basis for consistent and long lasting fun in the endgame.

The MegaMod is a project from the heart which has been going for over a decade now. The Mod always has been, and will always be, in a state of constant iteration and progress. Thanks to the complete rework of 'Reforged' it reaches
unprecedented levels of quality. Still, or actually because of that, this first iteration will be fraught with technical issues and bugs or inconsistencies in the overall design. Fixing all of these
will take time and a lot of effort and will only be possible with your help. There also always will be experimental changes and ideas, to push the boundaries of Modding in The Guild 2 just a little bit further. 
It's completely understandable that not every decision will be liked by everyone.

To pay tribute to the diversity of players and supporters of the project, with 'Reforged' there will be two versions of the MegaMod from now on. Besides the "full version" there's also a "lite edition." This edition 
will benefit from all technical improvements, bugfixes and quality of life changes just as much as the full version. However, more general overhauls of professions or gameplay will be absent
and reserved for the "full version." We hope that Reforged Lite (0.x) can be a substitute for official patches. You may use the files of the Lite Edition for your own mods
without asking. In case you want to use the full mod as a base, you should contact us first and build your modifications as an 'add on' without distributing the whole mod. 

We wish you a lot of fun with this little gem and with the Modpack!

--Overview of version 0.1 (Lite)--

- The mod has been redone from scratch, adding features of MMP 0.95 by hand onto a clean installation of Patch 4.211. As for now, not ALL features of the old mod have been added (yet).
- Due to the separation into 'Lite' and 'Full mod', AI changes and new items have NOT been added yet.
- Countless problems and bugs of Vanilla and MegaMod 0.95 have been solved or bypassed.
- There's new music, some new icons and character textures or model alterations.
- There's a new map (Yorkshire) to play on.
- The bigger overview-map and building upgrade-sheet from MMP 0.95 have been added.
- Trading routes have been reworked to an even greater extent than MMP 0.95. There's also some new actions to support your businesses.
- The favor-system has been reworked greatly and now includes a new 'Grudge' and 'Fondness' system, which modify the gains and losses of all favor on a dynasty-wide scale.
- Fame and imperial fame has been reworked.
- AI and players have roughly the same starting conditions, even on the highest difficulty settings.
- Overall you need more experience points to reach the higher levels. XP gains scale with the years-per-round setting.
- Every business can now have up to 16 item slots, but only the warehouse can have really big slots.
- The diplomacy feature of 0.95 has been added: it has has been reworked completely and got a lot of extras additions.
- The takeover bid feature of 0.95 has been added: make a offer for a building already owned by a dynasty, paying much more than its actual worth.
- A new, more dynamic system for the city treasury has been added.

... and much more!
