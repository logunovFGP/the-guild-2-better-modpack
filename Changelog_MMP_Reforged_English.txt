Installation:

Install The Guild 2 - Renaissance
Patch it up to Version 4.211 (Patch can be found at www.gilde2.de) unless you have the Steamversion (which is automaticly patched)
Delete the 'Scripts' folder in your main game directory
Extract the FMMP.zip to your main game directory, overwrite everything.
Make sure the language in your DB/text.dbt matches your language setting in your config.ini

Enjoy!

*** General ***

_Tech_
- Script and database cleanup: Improved the readability, functions and structure, removed redundant code, unified the script-design.
- Seperated unused scripts inside the new 'Removed' folder (To make use of that, you must delete your original Scripts folder before applying this patch).
- Newly hired workers will no longer get cured automaticly.
- The idle and freetime behavior of many NPCs has been altered either to improve performance or immersion.
- AI only needed to pay 40 zu 80 percent of the money players had to pay during certain measures. To fix this SpendMoney has been replaced by chr_SpendMoney
- Duels are a bit more fluent now.
- Officesession script has been replaced and restructured completely.
- Trial script has been reworked.

_Design_
- Instead of Germany, Hungary is now a possible enemy of Austria in war.
- Duels start a bit earlier and the message to attend them also happens earlier aswell.
- There are now 2 trials per round instead of one, beginning in the early morning hours. The second follows right after that.
- The icon for 'Application for lowest level post' has been removed
- Dynasty colours have been changed to darker and more distinctive tones. Badges have been adjusted aswell.
- The 'thinkbreak' where you could compliment and bribe during office sessions and trials has been removed. Reasons for that are balancing, bugs and the time it added to the sessions.
  You now have to prepare before the sessions start.
- State-Icon for 'pregnancy' now is shown on the left-below corner instead of right. Thus, active measusures of that character are not hidden by the pregnancy state symbol.
- Multiple icons have been reworked.
- Some character textures have been overhauled.
- The numbers of possible dynasties per map have been adjusted.
- Map duplicates have been removed. Thus multiple old maps, which I don't have the source files from to edit them, can now longer be played on.
- The message 'Someone feels neglected' has no voiceover anymore and the text has been changed.

*** Balancing ***

_General_
- They way talents impact your measures has been reworked completely. Instead of a linear progression there is a higher random factor and levels of succession. You will
  also get visual feedback every time your character attempts a skillcheck.
- You can now use ALL of your buisness inspectors to inspect buisnesses instead of just one at a time. However you can no longer inspect buisnesses more than once every 48 hours.
  Using that power will make you lose a lot of favor with the owner of that buisness and with their workers.
- Declining a bribe will now lose you only 5 favor.
- Duels are more dangerous.
- If you don't attend a duel, you will lose favor with every sim nearby of the duel place.
- If you remove someone's immunity you will lose some favor now.
- At gamestart workshops will be chosen random by AI.
- All coloured dynasties start with the same amount of gold.
- No matter how you set up the difficulty, you will start with at least 5000 gold.
- Shadow dynasties will try to live in residences now.
- The option to start with a family by changing the config.ini has been deactivated.
- Experience needed for levelups has been increased and more evenly distributed.
- If you remove pamphlets you get experience now.
- Requirements for nobility titles have been rebalanced and amount of buisnesses you can own has been drasticly reduced to improve human-ai balance and for better lategame performance.
- Building prices have been adjusted.
- Building upgrade prices have been adjusted and streamlined. Some buildings are more expensive than others.
- All workshops can have up to 16 inventory slots and start with 6 slots for free, except for level-1 buildings: Wood cutter, miller, miner, orchardist.
- Some workshops now have protection against burglary / fire which they don't had before.
- Workshops that are built inside the city have higher protection against burglary but less against fire. Outdoor-workshops vice versa.
- All workshops have now 4 possible worker slots, only gatherer-professions like farmer or miner and rogue professions get up to 5 employees. Those also start with 2 free employees instead of just 1.
- Chances of random city events have been adjusted
- The warehouse now has less upgrades against burglary but now can store much more goods (up to 16 slots, up to 400 units per slot)
- The class-talents of all 4 classes have been rebalanced.
- Costs of cart escorts have been reduced from 750 to 600 gold.
- Weight of evidence, their influence on favor and deposition has been rebalanced.
- Voters for offices have been changes to emphasise the distinction between the clerical and non-clerical 'chambers'
- The damage sims do by attacks on buildings has been increased significantly.
