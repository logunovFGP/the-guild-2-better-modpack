**Producer** = the building that manufactures the item (from `BuildingToItems` and the item's `manufacturer`
column) and, when a market stocks it, the market it spawns at with the town level needed and the units
placed at game start. **Used on** comes from the target filter of the item's `Use<name>` measure:
self, own building/cart, another dynasty's character or building, or a position; it decides
whether an item is a weapon against others or a buff for oneself. "Another dynasty's character"
also covers friendly gifts (Poem, Tart); the Effect column tells which. **Effect** is the in-game tooltip.
